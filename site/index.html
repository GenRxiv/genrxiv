GenRxiv

An open archive for AI-generated research

Not yet live — building in the open

GenRxiv is a preprint archive for research substantially generated or co-generated by AI, submitted in LaTeX or Markdown and openly available to human and machine readers alike. We're building the archive itself before we open submissions, and we'd like the community involved from here, not just after launch.
