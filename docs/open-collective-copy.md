## About / long description

GenRxiv is a preprint archive for research that was substantially generated
or co-generated by AI.

Preprint servers already exist for most fields — arXiv for physics and maths,
bioRxiv for biology, ChemRxiv for chemistry. None of them were built with
AI-generated work in mind, and most are still deciding what to do about it.
GenRxiv starts from the other end: it assumes the AI involvement and asks that
authors disclose it plainly, so the work can be read, cited, and evaluated with
that context in the open rather than guessed at.

**How it works**

- Submissions are accepted in LaTeX or Markdown and rendered to PDF and HTML.
- Authors identify themselves with an ORCID iD, so attribution is verifiable.
- Every paper carries a disclosure of what was AI-generated and how.
- Metadata is published over OAI-PMH, the same protocol the other preprint
  servers use, so anything indexed here is readable by humans and machines
  alike without scraping.
- Everything is open access under a Creative Commons licence.

GenRxiv is not peer-reviewed and does not evaluate the scientific quality of
submissions. It is a place to put work where others can find it.

**Where the money goes**

The archive currently runs on a single self-hosted server and a domain, funded
personally. Contributions cover hosting, storage and backups, the domain, and
eventually DOI registration so papers here get permanent identifiers. Every
expense is filed publicly on this page — you can see exactly what came in and
what went out.

**Getting involved**

We need more than money: moderators, people to help write the submission and
disclosure policy, and researchers willing to be early authors. If any of that
sounds like you, get in touch.

## Contribution tiers

**Reader — $5/month**
Keeps a month of storage and bandwidth running. Listed as a supporter if you'd
like to be.

**Contributor — $25/month**
Covers hosting for the month. Listed as a supporter, and an invitation to the
discussions where policy and roadmap get decided.

**Sustainer — $100/month**
Covers hosting and backups with room to grow. Listed as a supporter, invited to
policy discussions.

**Institutional supporter — $500/month**
For labs, departments, and organisations. Logo placement on the site, listed as
an institutional supporter, and a standing invitation to shape governance.

**One-time contribution — any amount**
Every bit helps and is spent on the same things.

## Goals

**Goal 1 — Keep the lights on: $40/month**
Covers the domain, off-site backups, and the running server.

**Goal 2 — Move off the home server: $150/month**
Puts the archive on proper hosting with real uptime, so it stops depending on
one machine and one internet connection.

**Goal 3 — DOIs for every paper: $300/month**
Funds DataCite or Crossref membership so every submission gets a permanent
identifier and can be cited properly.

---

## Expense policy

Funds are spent only on running the archive: hosting, storage, backups, domain
renewal, DOI registration, and preservation services. No one draws a salary.
Every expense is submitted with a receipt and appears on this page. If you want
something explained, ask — that's the point of doing it here.
